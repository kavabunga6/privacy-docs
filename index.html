Privacy Policy — Electric City

Last updated: July 10, 2026

Electric City ("the app", "we", "us") is developed by kavabunga6 (contact below). This app collects no personal information and makes no network requests at all.

Summary

Electric City does not require an account, shows no ads, and has no in-app purchases. It works entirely offline. Your gameplay progress (level, stars, achievements, settings) is stored only on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not operate any backend server, and the app does not communicate over the network in any way — it does not even request the Internet permission. All gameplay data — level progress, star ratings, unlocked achievements, and settings — stays in local app storage on your device. It only leaves your device if:

  • your device's own cloud backup (e.g. Android Auto Backup / Google One) copies it as part of a full device backup, or
  • you manually use the in-app "Export progress to file" feature to create a backup file yourself.

We do not use any advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, or payment SDK, and the app does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or photos.

Permissions the app requests

  • Vibrate — used for haptic feedback on taps and level completion. That is the only permission this app requests.

Children's privacy

Electric City is not directed at children and is not designed or marketed as a children's app. We do not collect personal information from children — or from anyone, since the app collects nothing at all.

Your choices

  • The entire game works offline; no feature requires an internet connection.
  • You can export and later re-import your progress yourself from Settings, independent of any store or cloud account.

Contact

Questions about this policy: devkondrashin@gmail.com

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the app's data handling ever changes. We'll update the "Last updated" date above when we do.
