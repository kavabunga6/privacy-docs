Privacy Policy — Electric City

Last updated: July 10, 2026

Electric City ("the app", "we", "us") is developed by kavabunga6 (contact below). This policy explains what data the app handles when you play it.

Summary

Electric City does not require an account and does not collect any personal information itself. Your gameplay progress (level, stars, achievements, settings) is stored only on your device. The app shows ads and offers a one-time in-app purchase to remove them; the advertising and payment SDKs we use handle a limited set of technical data as described below.

What we collect

We (the app developer) do not operate any backend server and do not collect, store, or sell any personal data ourselves. All gameplay data — level progress, star ratings, unlocked achievements, and settings — stays in local app storage on your device. It only leaves your device if:

  • your device's own cloud backup (e.g. Android Auto Backup / Google One) copies it as part of a full device backup, or
  • you manually use the in-app "Export progress to file" feature to create a backup file yourself.

What our third-party SDKs collect

  • Advertising (Google Mobile Ads / AdMob): shows interstitial ads between levels. Google's ad SDK may collect an advertising identifier and technical device/app information to serve and measure ads, per Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP): shows a consent form for ads where required (e.g. EEA/UK) before any ad request is made.
  • Google Play Billing (Google Play build) / RuStore Pay SDK (RuStore build): processes the optional one-time "remove ads" purchase. Purchase/transaction data is handled by the respective store's payment system, not by us directly.

We do not use any analytics or crash-reporting SDK, and the app does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or photos.

Permissions the app requests

  • Internet / network state — required to request ads and process purchases.
  • Vibrate — used for haptic feedback on taps and level completion.
  • Advertising ID — used by the ad SDK as described above.

Children's privacy

Electric City is not directed at children and is not designed or marketed as a children's app. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

  • You can play the entire game offline; ads and purchases require an internet connection, but gameplay itself does not.
  • The one-time purchase to remove ads is optional.
  • You can export and later re-import your progress yourself from Settings, independent of any store or cloud account.

Contact

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes (e.g. new SDKs). We'll update the "Last updated" date above when we do.
